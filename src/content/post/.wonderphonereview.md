wonder phone review

When I first heard about the wonder phone, I was a bit skeptical.

Another $300+ lockdown touchscreen flip phone? Who needed more of the same?

They tried marketing themselves as a replacement.  for the beloved LG Exalt, but come on, don't try telling me that they actually managed to make a simple  phone polished enough to  compare.

they advertised having android auto, and I guess that would be a feature that would get people, but I personally don't use android auto, so that didn't really talk to me.

A while later, I got my hands on a wonder phone, and I  completely changed my mind.
