:::[Verdict:]
One of the best options if you want a simple, reliable phone with some modern features.
:::

There are a lot of new dumb phones that try to get you with flashy features or creative ways of being minimalist.

Then there are some phones that don't stand out for being the most  exciting, but that's not what they're all about.

Instead, they focus on being the best, true, reliable, dumb phones, with some modern features added on.

That's what I found when I tried the Sunbeam F1 Pro: A phone that works well, and that you can depend on to be there and do it's job. 

It's been around for a while, you'll find many people who've used it and give good reviews on it, it works well, and won't suddenly start failing or bugging out on you.

But don't get me wrong, this phone isn't just a better HMD phone.
 It has many modern features, some of which are innovative and can be quite useful.
 One of it's latest features, NumberLink, allows you to pair your phone with their app to access your phone remotely. 
Once connected, you'll be able to read and respond to messages, and make and receive calls using your number, remotely.
The app is available for Android iOs, Linux Windows, and mac.

It has a simple, functional, UI that's easy to use, but doesn't encourage overuse, perfect for digital detoxing.
The UI is a big contrast to the flashy, animated UIs that smartphones have, but once you get used to a UI that just does what it's supposed to, and isn't trying to be your friend, you start appreciating having a phone that you can use intentionally, a phone that doesn't try to give you a dopamine hit every time you open the home screen.


It's apps are all built custom, work very well, and are fully dpad compatible.

It has a strong body, making it durable and long lasting.

It has options for Waze, email, hotspot, weather and their own navigation app.
Voice typing, NumberLink, and some features inside their weather and navigation apps, are all available as an optional subscription add-on.

One of the interesting things about this phone is that Sunbeam offers multiple variants, each offering different combinations of features like texting, media playback, and email, allowing you to choose an option that has what you need without any extra distractions. 

The phone gets periodic OTA updates, fixing bugs and adding features.

Another area that I think the  Sunbeam really shines is as a senior phone.
Everything is very simple and intuitive, without the complexity of smartphones.
It has a manual on the phone itself, clearly explaining every menu and feature.

---

### What I don't like about this phone

Although overall this phone is a great option, no dumbphone is without flaws, and the F1 Pro has it's drawbacks.

The camera isn't great. Although it's not any worse than the average dumbphone, I would expect better from a phone at this price point.

I don't either like that some features need a subscription, although I understand that those are features that cost them, and their subscription isn't very expensive.

Other than that, I think that the phone is a great phone, and one of the best options if you want a simple, reliable phone with some modern features.
