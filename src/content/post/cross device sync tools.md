Androids Quick Share and Microsofts Phone Link are popular apps for linking your devices, but they can have limitations.

They're both dependant on Googles invasive Play Services, and support limited platforms.

These open source alternatives are cross platform, and in many ways better than the proprietary ones.

### LocalSend

LocalSend is a free, open-source app that allows you to securely share files and messages with nearby devices that are on the same network, without needing an internet connection.

It works on Android, iOs, macOs, Windows, Linux.

It even has a web version, allowing you to send files to people without them having to install the app.

:::details[Features]
**Cross-platform** - LocalSend is available for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS.
**Secure** - End-to-end encryption ensures that only you and the recipient can access your files.
**No Internet Required** - Works completely offline. Your data never leaves your local network.
**Blazingly Fast** - Transfer files at the maximum speed of your WiFi network. No bandwidth limits.
:::

https://localsend.org/
