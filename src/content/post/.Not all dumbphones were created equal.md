When getting a dumbphone for the first time, the options can be confusing.

There's a wide variety out there, including phones as simple as they were 20 years ago, more modern ones offering some featrues or apps, and phones that are essentially dumbed down smartphones, and everything in between.

What they all have in common is that they all have features designed to encourage intentional use.


## The classic dumbphone

These phones are in many ways like the old school feature phones; no touchscreen, numberpad, predicitive T9 typing.
